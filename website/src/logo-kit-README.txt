SAWMILL LOGO KIT

The approved mark: a symmetrical forest-green sawblade with a bold black
outline, a cream S, and white dimensional blocking outlined in black.
The complete letter, including its depth and outlines, is centered on the blade.

SVG
  sawmill-mark.svg                 Primary mark.
  sawmill-mark-reversed.svg        Cream blade and green letter for dark backgrounds.
  sawmill-wordmark.svg             Primary mark with outlined sawmill lettering.
  sawmill-wordmark-reversed.svg    Light wordmark for dark backgrounds.
  sawmill-mono.svg                 One-ink forest-green mark with transparent cutouts.
  sawmill-mono-light.svg           One-ink cream mark with transparent cutouts.

PNG
  Every SVG has a transparent PNG counterpart.
  Marks are 1024 x 1024 pixels. Wordmarks are 2048 pixels wide.
  The primary mark also comes in 256, 512, and 2048 pixel square sizes.

USE
  Use SVG for websites and print when possible; it scales without losing detail.
  Use PNG when an application needs a raster image. Preserve the aspect ratio.
  Keep clear space of at least one S-stroke width around the sawblade.
  White and cream within the full-color mark are intentional; the area outside
  the sawblade is transparent. The mono variants use transparent cutouts.

COLORS
  Forest green  #214538
  Warm cream    #f5f0df
  White depth   #ffffff
  Black outline #000000
  Brand orange  #fa7844

The wordmark lettering is outlined Barlow Condensed. The i/l and l/l pairs
each have 1px of extra spacing at the native SVG size. No font installation
is needed to use these files. The font's license is included as font-LICENSE.txt.
